\chapter{Planned Schedule}

\section{Activities and Timeline}
The research activities are structured according to the six-stage experimental pipeline defined in Chapter 3, spanning a period of 12 months (November to October). The project is divided into five operational phases, detailed below along with their estimated duration and deliverables.

\subsection{Task Description}

\paragraph{Phase 1: Methodology and Data Setup (Nov - Feb)} \mbox{} \\
Focus on finalising the state-of-the-art review and the acquisition/cleaning of the clinical dataset.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} Finalised Chapter 2 and a clean, structured CSV dataset.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Phase 2: Predictive Model Development (Feb - Apr)} \mbox{} \\
Implementation, training, and hyperparameter tuning of the baseline classifiers (\gls{rf}, \gls{xgboost}, \gls{dl}).
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} Trained model binaries with optimised performance metrics.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Phase 3: Explainability Generation (Apr - Jul)} \mbox{} \\
Application of \gls{shap} and \gls{lime} algorithms to generate local explanation datasets for the test instances.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} Feature attribution database and visualisation prototypes.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Phase 4: Evaluation and Validation (Jun - Sep)} \mbox{} \\
Execution of quantitative testing (\gls{fidelity}, \gls{stability}) and clinical concordance assessment against medical guidelines.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} Chapter 4 (Results) and Statistical Analysis Report.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Phase 5: Dissertation Writing (Jan - Oct)} \mbox{} \\
Final synthesis of results, writing of discussion/conclusions, and preparation of the defense presentation.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} Final Dissertation manuscript and Defense slides.
\end{itemize}
